What to ask before you price a scaffold

Five details every scaffolding quote request should give you, before you pick up the phone.

For scaffolders in Sussex and HampshireSwipe →
1

The address and postcode

So you can check it is on your patch, and look at the street, parking and access, before you ring back.

2

How many storeys, and which sides

Front only, or front and back? Two storeys or three? What is it for: roof, chimney, painting or an extension?

3

A photo of the access

Side passages, conservatories, gardens, a neighbour's drive. One phone photo tells you more than ten minutes of questions.

4

Pavement or road, and who is asking

Going on the pavement may need a licence from the council. A homeowner, a builder or a roofer changes the job and the conversation.

5

When it is needed, and for how long

Monday or next month? Two weeks or two months? It sorts the urgent jobs from the ones that can wait.
