## Design conclusions

Keep the warm green/cream identity, a specific outcome, a concrete workflow illustration and Oscar’s identity. Put scope and running costs beside the price. Make the first contact shorter. Use real proof as it becomes available; do not copy competitors’ ratings, customer claims or visual assets.

The original site’s estimator permits a website plus its included form to total £1,440. The new concept avoids that selection flow; the preserved pricing page still needs correction if retained. The original quote form has a configured Formspree endpoint, but inbox receipt was not tested in the business review. Avoid describing the form as disconnected merely because fallback HTML contains placeholder wording.

## Evidence and limits

The original review checked the public site, source, documentation, demo behaviour and 14 routes. Mobile and desktop layouts were inspected. That establishes presentation and observable behaviour, not actual sales, private integrations or production reliability.

The competitive opportunity is a hypothesis: a specific, reliable implementation around the client’s existing process. Test it against actual alternatives mentioned in interviews and reasons customers buy.
