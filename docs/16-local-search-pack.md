## Google Business Profile draft

### Check eligibility first

Google only allows a Business Profile for businesses that make **in-person contact with customers** during their stated hours. Online-only businesses are not eligible. Pillion Web's site says the work is done remotely and no visit is needed. If Oscar does not meet customers in person (for example at their yard, office or home), a profile is likely to be refused or suspended, and creating one anyway risks the account.

- If Oscar does meet customers in person, set Pillion Web up as a **service-area business** with the address hidden.
- If not, skip the Business Profile for now. Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and the directories below still help. Revisit if face-to-face meetings become part of how Pillion Web works.

Check Google's current [Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) before starting, because the rules change.

## Directories and groups to approach

Prioritised for effort against value. Check each one is current before joining: networking groups change often. Oscar does every sign-up and any outreach. Use the standard wording above.

### Listings (free, do first)

1. **Bing Places for Business.** Bing results feed several AI answer tools. It can import a Google profile; if there is no Google profile, create it directly as a service-area business.
2. **Apple Business Connect.** Apple Maps and Siri. Free.
3. **Yell.com free listing.** Widely copied by other UK directories. Choose the option to hide the address.
4. **FreeIndex.** UK directory for service businesses; free basic listing.
5. **Thomson Local, Cylex UK and Hotfrog.** Lower value. Do these last, only if time allows, with identical details.

Skip paid "link building" packages and directories that charge for a dofollow link. They break Google's spam rules and can harm the site.

### Local business groups (relationships, referrals and genuine links)

1. **Chichester Chamber of Commerce and Industry.** The district council's August 2026 newsletter mentions its events and a drop-in hub at Chichester Enterprise Centre. Membership usually includes a member directory listing.
2. **Sussex Chamber of Commerce.** Runs a Chichester drop-in hub. Wider Sussex reach.
3. **Worthing and Adur Chamber of Commerce.** Fits the Worthing page.
4. **Hampshire Chamber of Commerce.** For Portsmouth and Southampton, later.
5. **Federation of Small Businesses (FSB).** Paid membership with a member directory and local events.
6. **Informal networking:** Chichester and Arundel small business networking (Avisford Park Hotel), My Networking Club Chichester, and any local BNI or 4Networking group. Confirm each is still meeting before going.

### Trade associations (understanding customers, not quick links)

These are bodies for Pillion Web's customers rather than for Pillion Web. Some have supplier or affiliate schemes; check the terms and cost before applying. Their public pages are also a good source of the questions members ask.

- Scaffolders: National Access and Scaffolding Confederation (NASC)
- Roofers: National Federation of Roofing Contractors (NFRC)
- Builders: Federation of Master Builders (FMB)
- Plumbers and heating engineers: Chartered Institute of Plumbing and Heating Engineering (CIPHE)
