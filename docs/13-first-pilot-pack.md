# First pilot pack

Working material for days 1 to 3 of [Start here](00-start-here.md): approve the pilot offer, line up conversations and run the first one. Everything here is a draft for Oscar to approve. Tandem is still a working name, so drafts use Oscar’s own name and the existing Quotehelm contact address.

Drafts are not permission to send. Each message needs Oscar’s explicit approval and a lawful contact route, as described in the [sales playbook](04-sales-playbook.md).

## 1. Pilot offer for approval

Based on the proposed pilot in [first offer and pricing](02-offer-and-pricing.md).

**Two-sentence version (draft):** I set up a quote form on your existing website that saves every enquiry and texts and emails it to you straight away, so nothing gets lost while you are on a job. It is a fixed-price pilot: one website, one form, tested with you before it goes live.

**Decisions Oscar needs to make:**

| Question | Options | Suggested starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup price | £345 (current Quotehelm price) or another figure | Keep £345 as the pilot hypothesis (D04) and record actual hours |
| Who pays software and texts | Client pays vendors directly, or Oscar charges a managed monthly fee | Client owns the accounts and pays vendors directly; support is optional (D05) |
| Support after the defect period | None, optional monthly support, or included for the pilot period | Optional; define hours, response times and what stops if cancelled before offering it |
| Correction period | For example 14 or 30 days | 30 days for agreed scope only |
| Lead time | Fixed days, or "agreed after the compatibility check" | Agree per job until the test build shows real timings |

**Included (draft):** one website, one enquiry form, a saved record of each enquiry, a text and email to one agreed number and inbox, client-approved wording, a controlled live test and a short handover.

**Excluded (draft):** multiple sites or branches, automatic price calculations, emergency answering, follow-up or marketing sequences, a bespoke CRM, unlimited changes.

Once approved, copy the [scope template](templates/scope.md) privately for each client and fill it in.

## 2. Who to talk to

Aim for five conversations with owners of small plumbing and heating firms around Chichester. In order of preference:

1. People Oscar already knows, or introductions from them.
2. Local partners who can make an introduction, such as web designers, accountants or merchants.
3. Cold email to limited companies only, after checking Companies House and the contact route (see the [sales playbook](04-sales-playbook.md)).

Keep the list itself outside this repository.

## 3. Draft messages

**Asking a contact for an introduction:**

> Hi [name], I am testing a small service for local trades firms: it makes sure every enquiry from their website reaches the owner by text and email straight away. I am not selling anything yet. I would like 15 minutes with a plumbing or heating firm owner to understand how they handle enquiries now. Do you know anyone who might be happy to chat? Thanks, Oscar

**Message to an owner after an introduction:**

> Hi [name], [contact] mentioned you might be open to a quick chat. I am looking at how small plumbing and heating firms handle enquiries from their website and phone, and where things slip through. It would take 15 minutes, by phone or in person, whenever suits you. I am not going to pitch you anything on the call. Oscar Ayton, oscarayton@getquotehelm.co.uk

For a cold email, adapt the example in the [sales playbook](04-sales-playbook.md) and include identity, contact details and an opt-out.

## 4. Running the first conversation

Open with: "Thanks for the time. I am trying to understand how enquiries reach you now and what goes wrong, so I do not build the wrong thing. There is nothing to buy today."

Then use the qualifying questions in the [sales playbook](04-sales-playbook.md), and ask to see the current process where possible. Listen more than you talk. Do not demo until the end, and only if they ask.

Close with: "Would it be useful if I showed you a working version once it is built? Can I come back to you with what I learn?"

Record notes privately in the [discovery template](templates/discovery.md) the same day.

## 5. After three conversations

Look for the same problem described by more than one owner, in their words. Update decisions D02 and D03 in the [decision register](10-decisions.md) with what was learned, including reasons people were not interested.
