# Oscar start here

Tandem’s first job is to sell and deliver one useful improvement to a real business: a website enquiry reaches the owner, is recorded and can be acted on. Use a paid pilot to establish demand, delivery capability and cost before expanding the catalogue.

The website concept is a sales starting point. The interactive example is fictional and does not connect to an automation service. The documentation supplies a plan; it does not mean the operating business is already configured.

## What is decided

- Working parent brand: **Tandem**, broad enough for more services and products later.
- First service to test: enquiry capture and phone/email notifications on an existing trades website.
- Initial market hypothesis: small plumbing and heating firms around Chichester and West Sussex, initially focused on scheduled work.
- Build on existing tools where suitable. Do not start by writing a custom CRM.
- Keep the original Quotehelm site available as reference while refining the new direction.

## What you need to decide

1. Whether interviews support that customer group and problem.
2. Which platforms you can reliably install on and what access is required.
3. The exact pilot scope, price, ongoing charges, support and ownership terms.
4. Whether Tandem is usable after domain, business-name and trademark checks.
5. Which hosting and form arrangements will support the eventual live site.

See the [decision register](10-decisions.md). A working name is not evidence of trademark availability.

## Your first seven days

| Day | Task | Finished when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Read the plan and choose one pilot offer | You can explain it in two sentences and list what it excludes |
| 2 | Identify five suitable owners and arrange conversations | You have specific conversations scheduled, with appropriate permission/contact routes |
| 3 | Map one current enquiry journey with an owner | You understand a real recent problem and the tools already used |
| 4 | Create a private practice environment | Test form, record store and controlled notification destinations are ready |
| 5 | Connect capture, storage, email and SMS | You can demonstrate a real test enquiry without customer data |
| 6 | Break and recover the workflow deliberately | Duplicate and failed-delivery scenarios are logged and recoverable |
| 7 | Review what you learned and narrow the offer | You have a test log, actual costs, a demo video and next interview dates |

This is a sequence, not a promise that all integration work takes seven days. Do not compress testing to meet an arbitrary deadline.

## The three deliverables that matter first

1. **A working test case:** a record is stored, notifications reach controlled destinations, and failure recovery is demonstrated.
2. **A clear written offer:** exact scope, dependencies, fees, running costs, acceptance and support terms.
3. **A paid pilot:** a suitable owner uses the workflow and helps measure what changes.

Useful early questions are “will someone pay?”, “can I deliver this twice?” and “what happens when it fails?”. More pages, tools and services can wait until those answers are clearer.
