A roofer needs a scaffold up on Monday and messages three firms on Friday afternoon. Whoever replies first with a price usually gets it.

What should a scaffolding quote form ask?

Only what you need to decide whether to price the job and how. You choose the questions. Most scaffolders want:

  • The address and postcode, so you can check the location before you ring back.
  • What the scaffold is for: roof work, a chimney, painting, an extension or something else.
  • How many storeys, and which sides of the building.
  • Whether it goes on the pavement or road, which may need a licence from the council.
  • When it is needed and roughly how long it will stay up.
  • Who is asking: a homeowner, a builder, a roofer or another contractor.
