What can an AI chat on a trades website do, and what can it not?

An AI chat can answer common questions and take details out of hours. It cannot price a job, it can get things wrong, and it does not replace you. Here is the detail.

By Oscar Ayton, Pillion Web. Published 10 November 2026.

What is an AI chat on a website?

A small chat window on your website that answers visitors’ typed questions automatically, using information about your business that you give it.

It works out of hours, when you cannot answer the phone, and can pass the visitor on to your quote form or phone number.

What can an AI chat do for a trades business?

Answer common questions, such as the areas you cover, the work you do and how to get a quote, and collect a name, number and job description when you are busy.

  • Answer the same handful of questions you get every week.
  • Point people to the quote form or your phone number.
  • Take a name, number and a description of the job out of hours.

What can it not do?

It cannot price a job it has not seen, it can get things wrong, and it does not replace you.

  • Prices. It should not give prices for jobs, because it cannot see the job. Set it to say you will quote after you have the details.
  • Mistakes. It can give a confident wrong answer, for example about an area you do not cover. Keep its information short and accurate, and check what it says.
  • Bookings. It cannot book you in unless it is connected to your diary, and most small firms are better off ringing back.
  • Trust. Many customers would rather talk to a person. It should say clearly that it is automated, and make it easy to reach you.

Does it need a privacy notice?

Yes. If it collects names, numbers or job details, that is personal data, and your privacy notice should say how it is used and kept.

Is an AI chat worth it for a small trades firm?

Often a short quote form does the same job more simply. A chat is most useful when you get lots of repeat questions from website visitors.

Start by checking what customers actually ask. If most enquiries are “can you quote for this job?”, a quote form that reaches your phone may be all you need. See what a quote form does.
