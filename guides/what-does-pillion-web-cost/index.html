What does Pillion Web cost?

Fixed prices from £225, agreed in writing before work starts. No VAT and no monthly fee unless you choose optional maintenance.

By Oscar Ayton, Pillion Web. Published 10 November 2026.

What does Pillion Web cost?

Prices start from £225. Every job has a fixed price, agreed in writing before any work starts.

There are three fixes: a quote form that sends enquiries to your phone, the right details on Google, and a new website. The prices page lists the price of each. You can start with one.

Is there a monthly fee?

No, not unless you choose optional maintenance. Everything I set up keeps working without it.

Some fixes use online tools that have their own running costs. Where they do, you pay those suppliers directly, in accounts that belong to you, and I tell you the cost before you say yes.

Do you charge VAT?

No. Pillion Web is not VAT registered, so the price you are quoted is the price you pay.

How do I pay?

Half when you say yes, and half before it goes live, once you have approved it.

What I set up is yours once it is paid for. Problems with the agreed work are fixed free for 30 days after it goes live.

What if I am not sure what I need?

Send your business name and website. I will reply with what I would fix and a fixed price for each, free and with no obligation.

Want a second pair of eyes?

Send me your business name and website and I will tell you what I would fix, with a fixed price for each. Free, no obligation. Ask for a quote or email oscar@pillionweb.co.uk.
