What matters for a coastal trades firm?

Wind and rain off the sea mean some enquiries arrive in a rush after a storm: slipped tiles, blown fencing, leaks. Those customers ring or message several firms at once and go with whoever answers first. A form that texts you straight away, with the address and the problem, lets you reply between jobs.

Many firms here cover both Bognor Regis and Chichester. If your Google listing, website and directory entries list different towns or old numbers, customers can ring the wrong number. Making them match is a small job that removes that risk.
