Questions from Chichester firms

No. It is for trades based in and around Chichester. That includes firms covering the nearby villages, the Manhood Peninsula, Bognor Regis, and jobs that cross the Hampshire border.

Which trades is this for?

Roofers, builders, electricians, plumbers and decorators. Kitchen fitters too. If your customers enquire through your website, it is likely a fit.

What will you change on the website?

Most firms start with one of three fixes: a quote form that texts and emails you, your Google reviews on the site with a review request after each job, or an AI chat box that answers common questions and sends you the enquiry. Booking links, address fixes, a small website rebuild and job tracking are there if you need them.

Do you need to visit the yard or the job?

No. You send the business name and website. I do the work on the site, and you approve it before customers see it.

A quote form, booking link or address fix is usually ready in 3 working days. Reviews and chat are usually ready in 5 working days. A website redesign takes about 10 working days once I have your content and access. Google and the directories can take longer to show an address change.
