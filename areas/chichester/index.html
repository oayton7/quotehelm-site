The patch around Chichester

Chichester firms rarely work only in the city centre. Customers search by town and postcode. These are the districts people actually use. Your patch can be smaller or larger than this. You set it.

  • PO18. Villages to the north and west, including Bosham, Boxgrove, Eartham, East Dean, Goodwood, Funtington and Nutbourne.

Questions from Chichester firms

Is this only for firms inside Chichester itself?

No. It is for trades based in and around Chichester. That includes firms covering the nearby villages, the Manhood Peninsula, Bognor Regis, and jobs that cross the Hampshire border.

Which trades is this for?

Roofers, builders, electricians, plumbers and decorators. Kitchen fitters too. If your customers enquire through your website, it is likely a fit.

What will you change on the website?

Most firms start with one of three things: a quote form that sends every enquiry to your phone by text and email, making your name, phone number and address match on Google and the main directories, or a new website with the quote form built in.

Do you need to visit the yard or the job?

No. You send the business name and website. I do the work on the site, and you approve it before customers see it.

How much does it cost?

Right details on Google is £225. A quote form that sends every enquiry to your phone is £345. A new website is £1,095, and it includes the quote form. Optional maintenance is £59 a month, with the first month free and 30 days’ notice to cancel. Every job has a fixed price agreed in writing before work starts.

How do I pay?

Half when you say yes, and half before it goes live, once you have approved it. What I set up is yours once it is paid for.

How long does it take?

Fixing your details on Google usually takes 3 working days, though Google and the directories can take longer to show the change. A new website usually takes about 10 working days once I have your content and access. For a quote form on your existing site, I agree the timing after a quick check of how your site is built.

No. The form can ask for a postcode, so you know where each job is before you ring back. You decide the patch.

Can you promise more jobs?

No. I can make the enquiry path clear and get the message to your phone. I do not promise a number of extra jobs. There are no published case studies yet.
