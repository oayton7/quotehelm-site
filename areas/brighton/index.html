What matters for a Brighton trades firm?

A lot of Brighton and Hove housing is terraced streets, converted flats and older buildings, often in conservation areas. Jobs vary a lot from street to street, so a vague enquiry such as “roof quote please” usually needs a phone call before you can say anything useful.

A quote form can ask the questions you would ask anyway: the postcode, whether it is a house or a flat, which floor, and whether the work is on the street side. That way the first call back is shorter and you can tell sooner whether the job suits you.
