Which parts of Worthing do you cover?

Worthing firms often cover the whole borough and cross into Adur and Arun. These are the postcode districts customers use.

  • BN11. Worthing town centre and the seafront.
  • BN12. Goring-by-Sea and Ferring.
  • BN13 and BN14. Durrington, Salvington, Broadwater, Tarring and Findon.
  • BN15 and BN43. Lancing, Sompting and Shoreham-by-Sea, in Adur.
  • BN16 and BN17. East Preston, Rustington and Littlehampton, if you go west.

You set your own patch. The quote form can ask for a postcode, so you know where each job is before you ring back.

What matters for a Worthing trades firm?

Worthing sits between Brighton and Chichester, so customers searching here often see firms from both directions. When people compare several local firms on their phone, the ones that are easy to contact and reply quickly tend to get the call back.

If your Google listing says Worthing but your website says Lancing, or shows an old mobile number, customers can be unsure whether you cover them. Matching details across your website, Google and the main directories removes that doubt.
